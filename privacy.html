Privacy Policy

YATO AI by YATO FX. Last updated: 11 October 2026

YATO AI is a chat assistant made by YATO FX. This page explains what information the app uses.

What we collect

  • If you sign in with Google: your name and email address, which Google shares with us when you tap Continue.
  • If you sign up with email: your name, your email address and a password. Your password is stored only in a scrambled (hashed) form and we cannot read it.
  • The messages, files and images you send in a chat, so the AI can answer you.

How we use it

  • To log you in and keep your account secure.
  • To send you verification and password reset codes by email.
  • To generate answers. Your messages are sent to an AI service that writes the reply.

What stays on your device

Your chat history, theme and settings are saved in your own browser. Signing out does not delete them; you can clear them from your browser settings.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your information.
  • We do not show you ads.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete your account at any time by emailing officialyatoai@gmail.com.
